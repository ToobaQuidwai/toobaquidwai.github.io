Decoding Cilia — from molecular architecture to disease

Cell and molecular biologist with 12+ years investigating ciliary biogenesis, cytoskeletal dynamics, and vesicular trafficking. Focused on understanding defective cilia-associated disease mechanisms — from kidney cysts and obesity to blindness and cancer. Expert in super-resolution microscopy (STORM, STED, SIM, PALM, CLEM), proteomics, and mouse genetics. Published in eLife, AJHG, and Chemical Science, with 258 total citations.
